Natural Motion Manifolds

Leveraging the geometry of the trajectory space to perform efficient agile locomotion



Presented by Gabin Lembrez

A non-linear conservative system


  • A classical template for legged locomotion with $n=3$ DOFs
  • Assumption: massless leg
  • Hybrid dynamics: $$ \left\{ \begin{matrix} \dot{x} = f_i(x) & x \in \mathcal{X} \\ x^+ = \Delta_i (x^-) & x^- \in \mathcal{S}_i \end{matrix} \right. $$

The problem


Agile legged locomotion is not limited to periodic limit cycles !


  1. How to maximize the use of intrinsic dynamics in locomotion ?
  2. How to perform a reversing maneuvre efficiently ?

Poincaré section and periodic orbits


  • Surface $\mathcal{S}$ of dimension $n-1$ transverse to the flow $x(t)$
  • First return map $P:\mathcal{S}\rightarrow \mathcal{S}$
  • In a neighborhood of a periodic orbit there are other periodic orbits (Raff & al., 2022)
Raff, M., Rosa, N. & Remy, C. D. (2022). Connecting Gaits in Energetically Conservative Legged Systems. IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters.

Natural Motion Manifold


A two-dimensional invariant sub-manifold $\mathcal{M}\subset \mathcal{X}$ is a Natural Motion Manifold if all evolutions on it are periodic and conservative (Calzolari & al., 2025).
Calzolari, D., Della Santina, C. & Albu-Schäffer, A. (2025). Exciting families of passive gaits in an elastic quadruped via natural motion manifold control. The International Journal of Robotics Research.

The Generator


  • Dimension one intersection of the NMM $\mathcal{M}$ with a Poincaré section $\mathcal{S}$
  • Fully describes $\mathcal{M}$
  • Parametrized by energy level: $$ \gamma (E) = \{x\in \mathcal{M} | a(x) = 0\}$$

Continuation of a family of motion


  • $\mathbf{z}_k$ is a periodic trajectory if $\mathbf{r}(\mathbf{z}_k)=\mathbf{0}$
  • Predictor step: take a small step in the tangent space of $\mathbf{r}$: $$ \left. \frac{\partial \mathbf{r}}{\partial \mathbf{z}} \right|_{\mathbf{z}_k}\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{0} \quad \longrightarrow \quad \overline{\mathbf{z}}_{k+1} = \mathbf{z} + \delta \mathbf{v} $$
  • Corrector step: Find the closest zero of $\mathbf{r}$ using Newton-Raphson

Bifurcation points


  1. Detection: the dimension of the tangent space is locally 2 $$ \det \left( \begin{matrix} \left. \frac{\partial \mathbf{r}}{\partial \mathbf{z}} \right|_{\mathbf{z}_k} \\ \mathbf{v}_k^T \end{matrix} \right) = 0 $$
  2. Continuation: projection of the equation $\mathbf{r}(\mathbf{z}^*)=\mathbf{0}$ in the plane $(\mathbf{\tau}_1,\mathbf{\tau}_2)$ to get the tangent to the new branch

Contribution 1


  1. Generalization of the method in Raff & al. 2022 to enable antiperiodic and period $>1$ motion ($\mathcal{T}_i$)
  2. Design of a collection of connected families of motion offline, through numerical continuation

Agile locomotion from motion primitives


  • Goal: perform a u-turn with as little energy variation as possible.
  • Idea: staying close to NMMs to maximize the use of passive dynamics.
  • Leverage the transition NMM

Actuators


  • Actuators are placed in parallel to the springs
  • $u_f$ is only active during flight
  • $u_s$ is only active during stance

Predictive control

Goal. Select the future behaviour of the robot in one step (stance-flight sequence) by switching to a different NMM in the collection

$$ \mathcal{L}_6^- \stackrel{\text{step 1}}{\longrightarrow} \mathcal{T}_2 \stackrel{\text{step 2}}{\longrightarrow} \mathcal{L}_6^+ $$

Let $\gamma (E)$ be a polynomial interpolation of the generator. Solve for $u$ over one stance-flight cycle ($N$ samples):


\[ \begin{align} \min_u &\quad \sum_{k=0}^N \| u_k \|_R^2 \\ \text{s.t.} &\quad \dot{x}_{k} = f(x_k,u_{k}) \\ & \quad e_i(x(t_i^-)) = 0 \\ & \quad \gamma(E(x_N)) = 0. \end{align} \]

Contribution 2


  • Successful two-steps reversing maneuvre
  • Compute time $\sim 0.01 s$ using IPOPT in CasADi
  • Near-optimal energy efficiency compared to not using the transition NMM

The control signals


  • Excitation of the form $$ A \cos(\omega t + \phi) + b$$
  • Small magnitude.
  • Behaves like a "non-linear" mode.

Discussion


  • Insight on the dual design problem: what stiffness yields the most efficient locomotion?
  • An alternative to reinforcement learning: rely on a priori knowledge and parcimony rather than exploration through sampling.

Perspectives


  • Generalize the approach to robots that dissipate energy by contact with the ground.
  • Move to three dimensional systems.
